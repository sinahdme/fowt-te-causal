# Data conventions

- Dataset: jacket-type offshore wind turbine foundation accelerometers (Dataverse DOI: 10.34810/data1011)
- Use ONLY `.tab` files with `sep="\t"` — the `.csv` files are truncated at 12.5 MiB and incomplete.
- Columns `sensor_1` ... `sensor_24` are in volts; multiply by 19.6 to convert to m/s².
